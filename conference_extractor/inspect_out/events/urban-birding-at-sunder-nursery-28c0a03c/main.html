Sunday, 23rd Nov
Bird Walk

With Ishtiyak Ahamad, Give Me Trees Trust
Please contact #8800623154

Time: 09:00 am to 11:00 am

Meeting point: Coffee Kiosk, Sundar Nursery

Rs 350/- per person (all age welcome)

Description

Join us for an exciting adventure as we explore the captivating world of birds on an upcoming Bird Walk at Sunder Nursery. This event promises to be a journey of discovery as we immerse ourselves in the rich bird ecosystem and habitats that call this historic park home. It's a wonderful opportunity to reconnect with nature and truly appreciate the beauty of our feathered friends.

About Organiser:

Ishtiyak Ahamad is a wildlife and biodiversity expert with an experience of more than 13 years, working with Give Me Trees Trust as a Manager | Nature Education and Training currently. He started his career in environmental field as an Education Officer in BNHS (Bombay Natural History Society) at Asola Bhatti Wildlife Sanctuary, Department of Forest & Wildlife, Delhi. His expertise ranges from wildlife, biodiversity, thematic gardens creation, nature walks and trails, landscaping to afforestation, trees and wetland. So far, he has organized and moderated more than 5,000 nature educational sessions, workshops and nature walks. What do you need to do:

  • Entry ticket charges to Sunder Nursery apply
  • Walk timing and slot will be shared by the coach
  • This photo walk slot is non-transferable/refundable
  • In case of cancellation, complete amount will be refunded
  • We will be using the event/walk pictures/videos for promotions

We also do workshops, photo tours for groups and schools. To know more email, us at info@sundernursery.org
