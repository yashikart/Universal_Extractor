Congress Scientific Awards
The Congress Scientific Awards are designed to help offset expenses incurred to attend the TTS 2026 Congress and are awarded based on the scientific merit of applicants. Therefore, recipients must attend the Congress in person in order to receive the grant. If an awardee is selected for a scientific award, but does not attend the Congress in person, the monies of the award will be forfeited. These awards are supported by both TTS and Dr. Sanjeev Kanoria.

2026 International Basic and Translational Science Mentee-Mentor Awards
The 2026 International Basic and Translational Science Mentee-Mentor Awards are supported The Transplantation Society (TTS), in collaboration with national and international societies, recognizing the critical contribution of basic science to the field of transplantation. Through the International Basic and Translational Science Mentee–Mentor Awards, TTS celebrates scientists whose work has advanced our understanding of transplantation biology while fostering the development of young investigators who represent the future leaders of the field.
Read more
TTS-ISOT La Renon International Basic and Clinical Sciences Mentee-Mentor Awards
Through the TTS-ISOT La Renon International Basic and Clinical Sciences Mentee-Mentor Awards, ISOT, TTS and La Renon, celebrate the contributions of basic and clinical science to the field of transplantation. The award recognizes the efforts of scientists who have advanced our understanding of transplantation science/immunobiology and/or treatment of transplant recipients and the young investigators who will be the future leaders in our field.

Welcome Message

Dear colleagues and friends,

We are thrilled to invite you to mark your calendars for the 31st International Congress of The Transplantation Society (TTS 2026), taking place from September 20 to 23, 2026, in the vibrant city of Sydney, Australia. This landmark event will bring together over 3,000 participants, including clinicians, scientists, trainees, and allied health professionals from across the globe, all united by a shared commitment to advancing the field of transplantation.

Continue reading

As a global organization representing over 5,000 members from 105 countries, TTS is proud to partner with the Transplantation Society of Australia and New Zealand (TSANZ) to host this extraordinary gathering. TTS 2026 promises to be an unparalleled opportunity for scientific exchange, professional development, and collaboration.

Throughout this four-day congress, you will have the chance to engage with cutting-edge research and clinical practice through state-of-the-art lectures, plenary sessions, workshops, oral presentations, poster sessions, and affiliated program components. A distinguished roster of experts will share their insights, ensuring that all attendees leave with the latest knowledge and tools to enhance their practice and research.

The most compelling reason for our gathering is to improve the lives of patients in need of life-saving transplants. By joining the international transplant community at TTS 2026, you will contribute to this vital mission and help shape the future of transplantation.

We look forward to welcoming you to Sydney in 2026, where together, we can make a significant impact on the world of transplantation.
Warm regards,

John J. Fung
TTS 2026 Congress Chair and TTS President
