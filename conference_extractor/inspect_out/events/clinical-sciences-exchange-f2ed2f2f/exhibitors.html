Thank you for your participation in the 31st International Congress of The Transplantation Society!

Exhibitor Information

This page highlights essential information and deadlines for exhibitors.

All detailed requirements, forms, rules, and service information are available in the Exhibitor Manual, which all exhibitors are required to review.

Exhibition Overview

  • Venue: ICC Sydney - Exhibition Centre
  • Exhibit Hall: Hall 1-2
  • Congress Dates: 20-23 September 2026
  • Expected Attendance: 3,000+ international delegates

The exhibition will be integrated with networking breaks, lunches, poster sessions, and the Welcome Reception.

Exhibitor Move-In & Show Dates

Move-In

  • Sunday, 20 September 2026: 08:00-17:30

Exhibit Hours

  • Sunday, 20 September: Welcome Reception (18:30-20:30)
  • Monday–Tuesday, 21 and 22 September: 09:30-18:00
  • Wednesday, 23 September: 09:30-14:00

Move-Out

  • Wednesday, 23 September: 14:00-20:00
Note: Detailed schedules are available in the Exhibitor Manual

Exhibitor Badges

Exhibitor registration includes:

  • 2 exhibitor badges per 9 sqm (100 sq ft)

Badges provide access to:

  • Exhibit hall
  • Welcome reception
  • Coffee breaks and lunches
  • Congress materials

Registration instructions will be sent by email in April 2026.
