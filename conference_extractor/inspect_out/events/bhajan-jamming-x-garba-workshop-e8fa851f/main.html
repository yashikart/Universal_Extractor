About The Event

Bhajan Jamming x Garba Workshop
Welcome to a mystical fusion of divine melodies and high-energy garba vibes at the Bhajan Jamming to Garba Workshop hosted by Sneh Garba Classes & The Divine Yatra ✨

📅 Date: May 23, 2026
🕖 Time: 19:00
📍 Venue: Vivianna Farm, Near Marigold Circle, South Bopal, Ahmedabad

Embark on a soulful journey where traditional Bhajans harmonize with the rhythmic beats of Garba in an exhilarating workshop designed to uplift your spirits and energize your body!

At Sneh Garba Classes, we embrace the beauty of cultural diversity and the joy of communal celebration. This event is a unique opportunity to immerse yourself in the sacred tunes of Bhajans while seamlessly transitioning into the vibrant world of Garba, a traditional Gujarati folk dance that symbolizes unity and festivity.

Join us for an unforgettable evening filled with spiritual resonance, joyful movements, and heart-connecting melodies. Whether you’re a seasoned dancer or a novice enthusiast, our workshop offers a welcoming space for all to revel in the richness of our cultural heritage.

Experience the enchantment of music and dance coming together in perfect harmony and discover the sheer bliss of collective expression. Let your soul sway to the rhythm of Bhajan Jamming to Garba!

Please note: Indian Traditional Attire mandatory…

Tickets are available on AllEvents – secure your spot now for an enchanting evening that promises to ignite your senses and ignite your soul. 🎶🕺🙏 #BhajanJamming #GarbaWorkshop #SnehGarbaClasses
