Connects at Hong Kong Partner Show
23
07
2025

After a few highly productive days at The Battery Show Asia, we are now turning our focus to the next stage—our own exhibition taking place next month, at the same time, same venue, and carrying on the tradition of our previous 16 successful editions.

As part of the Informa Solar Exhibition Series, The Battery Show Asia has once again proven to be a valuable opportunity to connect with buyers, exhibitors, chambers of commerce, and government representatives from across the region. We are especially thankful for the meaningful conversations with the Cambodia Chamber of Commerce, Department of Energy Philippines, and Germany Trade & Invest. These dialogues highlight the importance of regional cooperation and long-term engagement in shaping a resilient clean energy future.

Following these exchanges, we are optimistic that many of the partners and suppliers we met at the show will go on to contribute to projects not only in South Asia, where we’ve built strong foundations, but also in emerging Middle Eastern and European markets.

It was also a pleasure to reconnect with longstanding supporters—industry decision-makers and friends who have accompanied our platform over the years. As we prepare for next month’s event, we aim to deepen these connections and foster more grounded, results-driven collaboration—especially in a year of rapid change and growing energy demands.

There remain many critical topics to address, from energy storage challenges and policy uncertainties to real-world deployment of solutions across diverse regions. While we don’t often use words like “cutting-edge” lightly, we remain committed to advancing practical, future-ready solutions through the right mix of technology, partnerships, and shared insight.

We’re looking forward to seeing what conversations—and what deals—will take shape next month.

See you there!
