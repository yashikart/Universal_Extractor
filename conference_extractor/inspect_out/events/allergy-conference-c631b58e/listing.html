PAPER SUBMISSION

We only accept the paper/presentation title and author names for inclusion in the program. The title and author names you provide during payment on the Author Registration screen will be listed in the program. You may submit your title any time before the start of the conference; there is no deadline.

AUTHOR/LISTENER REGISTRATION

  • Invitation Letter
  • Certificate (after the conference has ended)
