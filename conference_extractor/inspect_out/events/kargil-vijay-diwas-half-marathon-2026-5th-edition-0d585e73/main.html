Kargil Vijay Diwas Half Marathon 2026 – 5th Edition

July 26 @ 5:00 am - 9:00 am

The Kargil Vijay Diwas Half Marathon (5th Edition) is more than a race — it is a tribute. Organised by Hindustan Marathon and held annually to honour the courage and sacrifice of Indian soldiers during the Kargil War, this running event invites participants from across the country to turn every stride into a salute to our brave heroes.

Scheduled for 26 July 2026 in New Delhi, the event coincides with Kargil Vijay Diwas — the day India commemorates its historic victory in Operation Vijay on 26 July 1999. Whether you are a seasoned runner or lacing up for the very first time, this is one race that means something far beyond the finish line.

Event Overview

Event: Kargil Vijay Diwas Half Marathon – 5th Edition

Date: 25–26 July 2026

Location: New Delhi, India

Venue: Vegas Mall, Dwarka Sector 14, New Delhi

Organised by: Hindustan Marathon

Run in Solidarity for Our Brave Heroes

Kargil Vijay Diwas, observed every year on 26 July, marks the day India reclaimed its peaks from Pakistani infiltrators in 1999. Over 650 Indian soldiers made the ultimate sacrifice during Operation Vijay — and this marathon exists to ensure their legacy is never forgotten.

When you run the Kargil Vijay Diwas Half Marathon, you are not just running for fitness. You are running in solidarity with the families of our fallen heroes, and in pride for a nation that stood firm against impossible odds.

Race Categories

21.1 KM – Half Marathon (Chip Timed) A competitive long-distance challenge for trained runners. Open to men and women aged 18 years and above. Award categories: 18–45 years | 45–60 years | 60 years and above

10 KM – Run (Chip Timed) Ideal for regular runners building stamina and race-day experience. Open to men and women aged 15 years and above. Award categories: 15–45 years | 45–60 years | 60 years and above

5 KM – Run (Chip Timed) Perfect for beginners and first-time race participants. Open to men and women aged 14 years and above. Award categories: 14–45 years | 45–60 years | 60 years and above

Top 3 male and female finishers in each age group receive awards.

Important Information

Bib & T-Shirt Collection Bib number and T-shirt collection is not available on race day. Collection details will be emailed to all registered participants 7 days before the event.

Refund Policy Registrations are non-refundable and non-transferable. In the unlikely event of cancellation due to unavoidable circumstances (natural or state-related), the run will be rescheduled — no refunds will be issued.

Medical Fitness By registering, participants confirm they are physically fit and adequately trained for their chosen distance. If you feel unwell or dizzy during the race, stop and call for aid immediately.

Route & Environment Dustbins will be placed along the route — please help keep the city clean. Traffic police will be deployed for participant safety throughout the course.

Race Results & Photos Photos, results, and certificates will be shared via email within 3 days of the event.

About Kargil Vijay Diwas

On 26 July 1999, the Indian Armed Forces successfully concluded Operation Vijay, recapturing every peak infiltrated by Pakistani soldiers in the Kargil, Dras, and Batalik sectors of Jammu & Kashmir. The operation lasted nearly three months under extreme terrain and weather conditions. More than 650 Indian soldiers laid down their lives in defence of the nation’s territorial integrity.

Kargil Vijay Diwas is observed every year to honour their supreme sacrifice and to remind the nation of their unmatched valour. Running on this day is the community’s way of saying — we remember, we are grateful, and we will never forget.
