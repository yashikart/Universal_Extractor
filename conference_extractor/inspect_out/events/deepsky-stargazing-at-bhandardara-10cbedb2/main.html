Stargazing & Camping at Bhandardara

Looking for the perfect stargazing place near Mumbai with clear skies and dark surroundings? Stargazing at Bhandardara with Stargazing Mumbai offers a spectacular view of the night sky just a few hours from the city.

Set amidst the serene Sahyadri ranges and away from city lights, Stargazing at Bhandardara lets you witness planets, star clusters, nebulae, and the Milky Way galaxy under crystal-clear skies.

Enjoy an overnight stargazing and camping experience, reconnect with nature, and soak in the wonders of the universe. This starry night at Bhandardara promises an unforgettable stargazing experience near Mumbai.

Ghatghar Village, Bhandardara


₹ 2650/ person

23rd -24th May 2026

EVENT HIGHLIGHTS

  • Experience stargazing and learn about constellations with Stargazing Mumbai experts.
  • 15+ Large Professional telescopes used in Stargazing Events.
  • Observe through the largest 16″, Sky-watcher 14” and 12” Goto Dobsonion mount telescope.
  • Watch Sunspots, prominences, sun filaments, solar flares, and the granular surface of the Sun through the Special Solar H-alpha Telescope.
  • Witness 30-35 deep sky objects throughout the night.
  • Have engaging discussions with Astrophysicists.
  • Enjoy Stargazing and Camping amidst the scenic ranges of Sahyadri under the clearest skies.
  • Learn basics of Mobile Astrophotography.

ABOUT VENUE

Stargazing at Bhandardara comes with many treats especially for trekkers and hikelovers. The campsite provides routes to various eye-catching places to visit during the day. Some of them being the historic Ratangad Fort, Konkan Kada and the Sandhan Valley. Then, after a long day, you may come to unwind, away from the city buzz, under the stars.
Who said the perfect holiday doesn’t exist?

GENERAL INSTRUCTIONS

  • Carry your id proof like aadhaar card, pan card, driving licence.
    Carry warm cloths (Jackets, sweaters, shawl). The temperature considerably (13-14 Degrees) drops during the night time.
  • The Campsite is close to the forest area so please wear socks and shoes if possible.
  • Carry a torch, you will need it in the dark. Also don’t forget to cover it with red cellophane paper (gelatin paper).
  • We will advise you to carry some snacks and soft drinks since you won’t be able to buy anything around the field. Please carry drinking water bottle, you will be able to refill the water.
  • Carry your medication and mosquito repellent cream.
  • Carry a Diary and Pen in case you would want to make note of things.
  • Drinking and smoking in the observation field are strictly prohibited.
  •  Tents other than the ones set up by us are not allowed. Please do not carry any personal tents of your own.
  • Basic washroom facility is available at campsite.
  • This is an edutainment program. All are requested to maintain the spirit of the program and co-operate. No unruly behaviour toward fellow participants will be tolerated.
  • Parking facility for private vehicles are available.
  • No one should wander off the field. Do not visit the forest area on your own even during the day time.
  • Any kind of misbehaviour which causes a disturbance to other participants will not be tolerated and immediate action will be taken against the person or the group.
  • An important point to remember Stargazing Mumbai is a voluntary base group of some Amateur Astronomers, our aim is to spread and popularise Astronomy.
  • We do not call unless requested from your side. We have official email id: stargazingmumbai@gmail.com and phone number: + 91 9112662662, + 91888988422. These are the only mode we will use for contacting you. Please be aware that we will not be calling/ emailing from any other number/ email id.

  • 8:00 – 9:00 pm: Constellation Session I
  • 9:00 – 10:00 pm: Telescope Viewing I
  • 10:00 – 11:00 pm: Dinner Break
  • 11:00 – 12:00 pm: Astronomical Discussion/ PPT with Expert
