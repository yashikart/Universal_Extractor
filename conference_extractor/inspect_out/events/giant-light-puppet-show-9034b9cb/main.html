Giant Light Puppet Show

Details

Jun 13 2026 to Jun 13 2026 1 p.m.

Price: Rs 499 Book/Buy

Where

Dr B.R. Ambedkar Bhavan

Millers Rd, Near Bhagwan Mahaveer Jain Hospital, Kaverappa Layout, Vasanth Nagar, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560052

Event Description

GEMS KIDS CLUB proudly presents the world-famous Giant Light Puppet Show – a 1 hour 30 minutes show with a 15-minute interval that will amaze both kids and adults.

This is not a normal puppet show. It is a fun, surprising, and completely wordless show filled with glowing light puppets. You will see giant puppets from 1 to 8 meters tall, beautiful 3D light figures, bright neon creations, and magical characters that are created live in front of your eyes. Some of them even fly above the stage and over the audience!

The show is created by internationally famous puppeteer Nikolai Zykov, who has performed in more than 50 countries and won many international awards. His shows are loved all over the world and always receive standing ovations.

This show fills theatres everywhere it goes. It is rare. It is unique. And it is unforgettable.
