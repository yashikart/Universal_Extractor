Publication:

1. Accepted papers will be published into ICEIT 2027 conference proceedings by IEEE. The proceedings will be submitted and reviewed by the IEEE Xplore and Ei Compendex and Scopus after the conference.

View ICEIT Publication History: https://www.iceit.org/pub.html

2. Selected papers from Track 3 and Track 4 will be included in the Edited Volume "Exploring Innovations in Educational Technology", which has been accepted by Springer. Final publication will be contingent upon Springer's review of the manuscript. Those papers will be sent to indexing database like: Scopus, ACM Digital Library, ERIC, INSPEC, Norwegian Register for Scientific Journals and Series, SCImago.

3. SCIE & Scopus Journal

Selected registered papers will be recommended and invited to publish an extended version on the following journals. Please make sure you have added at least 30-40% of a new contents.

a. FER - Future in Educational Research < Indexing: DOAJ>

b. Education Sciences （1区，JCR - Q1）<Scopus, ESCI (Web of Science), Educational Research>

c. IJIET - Int. Journal of Information and Education Technology <Scopus, INSPEC, UGC-CARE List, CNKI, EBSCO, Google Scholar>

d. IJLT - International Journal of Learning and Teaching <Google Scholar; Crossref, CNKI>

For more information, please visit: https://www.iceit.org/


Submission:

1. Submissions must be original and should not have been published or under consideration for publication elsewhere while being reviewed by ICEIT 2027.

Submission system: https://www.easychair.org/conferences/?conf=iceit2027

2. Official language is English in paper writing and presenting.

3. Track 1, 2, 5, 6 (At least full 4 pages)

   IEEE Template Word: https://www.iceit.org/file/instruct8.5x11x2.doc

   IEEE Latex Template: https://www.iceit.org/file/ieee-conference-latex-template.zip

4. Track 3, 4 (10 pages (Springer Template Word (At least full 8 pages): https://www.iceit.org/file/Microsoft+Word+Proceedings+Templates.zip

5. Full paper (publication and presentation) | Abstract (presentation)

More detail about submission, please visit at https://www.iceit.org/sub.html
